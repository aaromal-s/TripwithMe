About the Trip

Explore the majestic peaks and serene valleys of Himachal Pradesh. This 6-day journey takes you through the colonial charm of Shimla, the scenic beauty of Kullu, and the adventurous landscapes of Manali.

Itinerary

Day 1 & 2: Shimla

Arrival & Colonial Charm

Arrive and transfer to Shimla. Stroll along the Mall Road, visit the historic Christ Church, and enjoy panoramic views from Jakhu Hill.

Day 3: Kullu Valley

River Rafting & Scenic Drives

Drive towards Manali via Kullu. Stop for thrilling river rafting on the Beas River and visit traditional shawl weaving factories.

Day 4 & 5: Manali

Adventure & Snow Point

Explore Manali. Visit the ancient Hadimba Temple. Take a full-day excursion to Solang Valley or Rohtang Pass for snow activities.

Day 6: Departure

Farewell Himalayas

Morning breakfast surrounded by mountains. Transfer back for your onward journey with unforgettable Himalayan memories.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the package?

The package includes 3-star or 4-star accommodation, daily breakfast, private AC transfers between cities, and all guided sightseeing tours. Flights and monument entry fees are excluded.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before departure. Cancellations within 7 days incur a 50% fee. No-shows are non-refundable.

Is this trip suitable for children?

Yes, Rajasthan is a very family-friendly destination. We can arrange child seats for the vehicle and specific kid-friendly activities like puppet shows and light-and-sound shows.
